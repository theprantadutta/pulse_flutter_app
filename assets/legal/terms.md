## Router control

Only sign in to routers you own or are allowed to manage. Changes made from Pulse, such as turning a Wi-Fi network off, changing a Wi-Fi password or the internet connection type, or restarting the router, take effect on your router like changes made on its own page: devices may disconnect and the internet may stop until the setting is put back. Pulse asks before each change and marks changes that are not yet confirmed for your router model as "Try it", but routers and firmware differ and a change can fail or behave differently than expected. Routers usually allow one admin at a time: signing in from Pulse can sign out the router's page or another device, and Pulse asks before doing that. Router results that others reported are a guide, not a guarantee.

## Outside services

Public IP and location lookups use Pulse's own server (pulse.pranta.dev), with IP geolocation by DB-IP (https://db-ip.com, CC BY 4.0); locations are approximate and may be wrong. Speed tests use Cloudflare and Hetzner, and update checks use GitHub. These services may be slow, limited or unavailable at times, and the outside ones apply their own terms. Speed tests can use a lot of data, which may count against a mobile data plan. The Geo IP map is built into the app and needs no outside service.
